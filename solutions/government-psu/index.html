The challenges

What we hear from teams like yours

Data sovereignty is non-negotiable

Citizen and employee identity data may need to remain within India and, in some environments, within the organisation's own data centre. This makes deployment flexibility and control over identity infrastructure important considerations for government and PSU environments.

Mixed application estates

Government departments and PSUs often operate new portals, NIC-hosted systems and legacy client-server applications alongside each other, making consistent authentication and access control difficult across the application estate.

Contractor and transfer churn

Frequent transfers, deputations and contractor rotations can leave active accounts and outdated permissions across multiple systems unless access changes are managed as part of the identity lifecycle.

CERT-In requirements and public-sector security expectations make appropriate log retention, incident reporting, access controls and audit trails important considerations. An on-premises IamLogic deployment keeps identity data and audit logs within your infrastructure.

FAQ

Common questions

Is IamLogic an indigenous product?

Yes. IamLogic is designed, developed and supported from Bengaluru, India by an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified organisation.

Can it operate in an air-gapped or restricted network?

Yes. Access Manager and IamLogic IGA are designed for on-premises operation and do not require outbound internet connectivity to function. Talk to us about your specific network topology.

Can IamLogic be deployed entirely on-premises for government departments and PSUs?

Yes. Access Manager and IamLogic IGA support on-premises deployment, allowing identity data, credentials and audit information to remain within the organisation's infrastructure.

Can IamLogic manage legacy government applications?

Yes. Access Manager supports legacy application SSO through its browser plugin where modern federation such as SAML or OIDC is not available.

How does IamLogic manage contractor and deputed-staff access?

IamLogic IGA supports time-bound and just-in-time access so temporary users can receive approved access for a defined period and have it automatically expire.

Does IamLogic support access reviews for government and PSU environments?

Yes. IamLogic IGA supports access certification campaigns with review workflows and retained evidence, helping teams demonstrate who reviewed access and what decisions were made.
