The challenges

What we hear from teams like yours

Contractors and shift workers

Third-party maintenance staff, seasonal workers and long-term contractors need access that is easy to grant, appropriately scoped and automatically removed when it is no longer required.

Design and process IP

CAD repositories, PLM systems and other engineering applications contain sensitive design and process information. Access needs tightly defined roles, appropriate approvals and periodic review.

Plant-floor practicality

Authentication needs to work across shared terminals, rugged devices and plant-floor systems — not just standard corporate laptops — while maintaining individual accountability and appropriate access controls.

Manufacturers pursuing or maintaining ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification need to show that identities, access rights, authentication and privileged access are controlled across both corporate and plant environments. That includes the contractors and seasonal workers who come and go, and the PLM and CAD systems that hold design IP. IamLogic IGA and Access Manager support these requirements with joiner-mover-leaver automation, time-bound contractor access, RBAC, MFA and periodic access certifications. Every approval, review and change is logged, giving your team system-generated evidence for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 audits instead of manually assembled records. FAQ

Common questions

We have very old shop-floor software. Can it be covered?

Usually yes. The browser plugin can provide SSO for legacy web interfaces, while non-API applications can be provisioned through ticketing-workflow integration in IamLogic IGA.

What should manufacturers look for in IAM software?

Manufacturers should consider SSO, MFA, RBAC, legacy application support, contractor lifecycle management, access reviews and integration across corporate and plant applications.

Can IAM support contractor and temporary worker access in manufacturing?

Yes. Time-bound access and lifecycle controls can be used to grant contractors the access they need and automatically expire it when the defined access period ends.

Can IamLogic provide SSO for ERP and PLM applications?

Yes. Access Manager supports SSO across applications including ERP and PLM, while legacy applications can be brought into the access layer through the browser plugin where applicable.

How can manufacturers manage access to design and engineering systems?

RBAC, access reviews and identity governance can help restrict access to design and engineering applications based on roles, responsibilities and business requirements.

Can manufacturing IAM work with Active Directory?

Yes. Access Manager supports Active Directory integration and RBAC, allowing access structures to reflect plant, line, department and functional roles.

How does IAM help with shared plant-floor terminals?

IAM can provide controlled authentication and individual accountability across shared terminals, while MFA methods can be selected based on the requirements of the plant environment.

Can manufacturing access reviews cover contractors as well as employees?

Yes. IamLogic IGA can govern external populations such as contractors, allowing access reviews and lifecycle controls to extend beyond the permanent employee population.
