The challenges

What we hear from teams like yours

Speed vs. control

Product teams adopt SaaS tools quickly; security teams then inherit growing access sprawl, inconsistent permissions and the audit questions that follow.

First serious audits

An RBI inspection or bank-partnership due diligence can suddenly require access reviews, segregation of duties and audit evidence that has not previously been formalised.

Lean IT teams

Lean IT teams often do not have the capacity for manual provisioning, access changes and recurring spreadsheet-based access reviews.

RBI's Master Direction on IT Governance and scale-based regulatory expectations make access control, MFA and audit trails important considerations for NBFCs. Establishing identity governance early can help organisations build appropriate controls before regulatory or business requirements become more demanding. FAQ

Common questions

We're only around 200 people. Is IamLogic overkill?

No. Licensing starts at 25 users and the platform scales with your organisation, allowing identity controls to grow as your team and regulatory requirements develop.

Should a fintech company choose cloud or on-premises IAM?

IamLogic supports cloud, on-premises and hybrid deployment. Organisations can select the deployment model based on their application environment, security requirements and regulatory obligations.

What should fintech companies look for in IAM software?

Fintech companies should consider IAM software that supports SSO, MFA, application integration, identity lifecycle management, access reviews and audit evidence. IamLogic combines Access Manager and IamLogic IGA to address authentication, access and identity governance requirements.

Can IamLogic support RBI-focused access governance for NBFCs?

Yes. IamLogic combines MFA, access reviews, identity lifecycle management, segregation-of-duties controls and audit trails to help NBFCs manage identity and access requirements relevant to RBI assessments.

Can IamLogic provide SSO for SaaS applications used by fintech teams?

Yes. Access Manager supports SAML, OIDC and OAuth2 for centralised authentication across supported SaaS and business applications.

How does IamLogic automate employee onboarding and offboarding?

IamLogic IGA can connect identity lifecycle workflows to your HR system so employee joining, role changes and exits trigger the appropriate access changes across connected applications.

Does IamLogic support access reviews and segregation of duties?

Yes. IamLogic IGA supports scheduled access certification campaigns, reviewer sign-off and SoD policies to help organisations review access and identify conflicting entitlements.

Can IamLogic support fintech applications that do not have standard APIs?

Yes. IamLogic IGA supports provisioning workflows through ticketing integrations for non-API applications, while Access Manager provides legacy web application SSO through its browser plugin.
