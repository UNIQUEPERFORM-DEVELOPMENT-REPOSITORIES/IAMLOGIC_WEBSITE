Engagement

How an engagement works

  • 1. Assess. We review your applications, identity sources, current processes and the regulations that apply to you, and agree the scope.
  • 2. Design. We design the target setup — integrations, policies, roles and the rollout plan — and agree it with your team.
  • 3. Implement. Our engineers deploy and configure the platform and connectors, then roll out in phases to avoid locking users out.
  • 4. Hand over. We train your administrators, document the setup and hand over day-to-day operation — or keep running it for you as a managed service.
