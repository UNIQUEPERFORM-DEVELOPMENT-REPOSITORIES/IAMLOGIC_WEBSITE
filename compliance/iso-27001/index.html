Privileged access

Privileged access under ISO 27001 (A.8.2)

Annex A control 8.2 asks that the allocation and use of privileged access rights is restricted and managed. In practice, auditors look for privileged access that is granted deliberately, limited to what the role needs, reviewed regularly and backed by stronger authentication.

Restricted allocation

Elevated access requested and approved through defined workflows, with time-bound grants and segregation-of-duties policies in IamLogic IGA.

Regular review

Privileged entitlements included in access certification campaigns, with reviewer decisions and revocations recorded as audit evidence.

Stronger authentication

MFA and passwordless WebAuthn for privileged sessions, with adaptive step-up in Access Manager (A.8.5).

Protected credentials

Credential vaulting with automatic rotation for legacy and shared accounts, so privileged passwords aren't known or reused (A.5.17). See how this works in our privileged access case study.

What does ISO 27001 require for identity and access management?

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A groups its identity requirements into access control policy (5.15), identity management (5.16), authentication information (5.17), access rights (5.18), privileged access rights (8.2) and secure authentication (8.5). Auditors typically ask how access rights are granted, reviewed and removed, how authentication is protected, and where the evidence is.

What does ISO 27001 control A.8.2 privileged access require?

A.8.2 requires that the allocation and use of privileged access rights is restricted and managed. That usually means granting elevated access through approval, limiting it in time and scope, reviewing it regularly and protecting it with stronger authentication.
