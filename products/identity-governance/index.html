FAQ

Identity Governance FAQs

What drives identity lifecycle events?

Authoritative sources can include HRMS platforms, databases, files or APIs through the Python connector framework. External populations can use their own systems of record.

How do you provision apps without APIs?

For applications without APIs, IGA can use ticketing workflows such as ServiceNow or Jira, with completion documented and governed.

Can IGA work independently of Access Manager?

Yes. IGA can operate independently. When used with Access Manager, authentication signals can enrich governance decisions and governance outcomes can inform access enforcement.

Does IGA support on-premises deployment?

Yes. IGA supports on-premises, private cloud and hybrid deployment, with high availability and disaster recovery.

How long does IGA deployment take?

Initial rollout can be completed in weeks; full coverage depends on application count, integrations and onboarding requirements.
